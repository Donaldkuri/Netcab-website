About NETCAB

NETCAB is a telecommunications company based in Kokopo, East New Britain, Papua New Guinea. We specialise in networking and cabling, voice and data solutions, VSAT connectivity, and PABX systems for businesses, organisations, and institutions across the region.

Our Mission

To provide dependable, high-quality telecommunications infrastructure that keeps businesses in East New Britain connected — from initial setup to ongoing support.

Coverage Area

We proudly serve businesses and institutions throughout Kokopo and the wider East New Britain province.

Why Choose NETCAB?

  • Local expertise in East New Britain
  • Reliable, professional installation
  • Full range of telecom services under one provider
  • Ongoing support and maintenance
