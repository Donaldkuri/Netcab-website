Welcome to NETCAB

NETCAB delivers reliable networking, cabling, voice and data, VSAT, and PABX solutions to businesses and organisations across Kokopo and East New Britain, Papua New Guinea. Whether you're setting up a new office or upgrading your existing infrastructure, we provide dependable telecommunication services tailored to your needs.

Our Core Services

Networking & Cabling

Structured cabling and network infrastructure for offices and organisations.

Voice & Data

Integrated voice and data solutions to keep your business connected.

VSAT

Satellite connectivity for sites that fixed-line networks cannot reach. We supply, install and configure VSAT equipment so remote offices, projects and institutions stay online.

PABX

Business phone systems that manage internal and external calls. We supply, install and configure PABX systems, including extensions and call routing, for your organisation.

Starlink Router Installation

Professional Starlink setup. We position the dish with a clear view of the sky, run and secure the cabling, and set up the router and Wi-Fi so your site is connected quickly.
